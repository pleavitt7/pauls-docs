XSpeeds · qBittorrent · DiskStation

One indexer, one seed copy, one verified hand-off

A private tracker supplied the release, qBittorrent pulls it over the swarm, and the files stay on the NUC so they keep seeding. A separate worker copies the finished torrent to the DiskStation over SMB, compares every byte by SHA-256, and refuses to overwrite a file it did not write. Nothing is moved or deleted from the seed copy, and the NAS share is never mounted into the client.
