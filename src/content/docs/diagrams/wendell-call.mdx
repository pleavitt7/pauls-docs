## System overview

A call is answered by a live voice model. Booking work is handed off to a narrow
backend agent, and that agent is the only part of the system holding Google
Calendar access — the public voice profile stays tool-disabled.

## Routes

Twilio posts the call to `/incoming-call` through the ngrok tunnel. The bridge
checks the caller against the allowlist, then answers with media-stream TwiML that
carries the caller number and call SID, and Twilio opens the media websocket. Audio
streams to the live voice model in both directions while the bridge records the
call.

## Booking

The initial public booking handoff starts from a 1200 ms settled-transcript
watchdog rather than waiting on a native delegation notice; later turns use native
delegation. The backend reads calendar availability, offers a slot, and only writes
once the caller confirms out loud.

## Deployment notes

the reduced-motion frame, and the no-JavaScript frame all carry the complete
