Wendell is the voice assistant behind the Twilio number. This sequence covers one
inbound call, from the webhook that opens it to the calendar event that closes it.
The figure plays the six stages as you arrive; its controls step, pause, and replay
the call, and the keyboard works too — left/right arrows, `Home`/`End`, `Space` to
play or pause, and `R` to replay.

## System overview

A call is answered by a live voice model. Booking work is handed off to a narrow
backend agent, and that agent is the only part of the system holding Google
Calendar access — the public voice profile stays tool-disabled.

## Routes

Twilio posts the call to `/incoming-call` through the ngrok tunnel. The bridge
checks the caller against the allowlist, then answers with media-stream TwiML that
carries the caller number and call SID, and Twilio opens the media websocket. Audio
streams to the live voice model in both directions while the bridge records the
call.

## Caller memory

Once the caller is on the allowlist, the bridge derives a bank name from the trusted
`From` number and recalls only that bank from the local Hindsight service. The bank
never reaches GPT-Live and is not the `wendell` profile's memory, which stays off. A
save needs a second explicit confirmation from the caller, and secrets and
transcripts are refused.

## Booking

The initial public booking handoff starts from a 1200 ms settled-transcript
watchdog rather than waiting on a native delegation notice; later turns use native
delegation. The backend reads calendar availability, offers a slot, and only writes
once the caller confirms out loud.

## Deployment notes

The plate is a self-contained HTML artifact under `public/diagrams/`. Its motion
controller is the canonical one from the diagram-design skill, so the static frame,
the reduced-motion frame, and the no-JavaScript frame all carry the complete
sequence. Embedded it follows the site theme and fonts; opened on its own it keeps
its own paper, ink, and typefaces.
